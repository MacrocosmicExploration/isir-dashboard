WHAT THE TRANSFORM DOES
- Requires the core columns verified in the supplied schema. Missing required
  columns cause a clear error; missing optional workflow fields become null.
- Preserves all source rows and both SharePoint ID and SourceItemID.
- Uses curated Incident Category and Lost... fields. AI fields are not selected.
- Does not filter by country, subsidiary, CaseState, or ConsistencyStatus.
- Derives SubsidiaryCandidate from the first token of User at Fault. It does not
  validate the token against a complete subsidiary roster or infer department.
- Extracts Incident Date, City, State, Country and Place of Work from plain-text
  snapshot lines with exact English labels and a colon. Matches ignore case.
- Keeps workplace separate from snapshot city. Geographic labels remain
  candidates until the source owner confirms what they mean.
- Accepts year-first numeric dates such as 2026-02-09 and 2026/2/9. It rejects
  five-digit years, impossible dates, duplicate labels, and ambiguous date order.
- Does not substitute SubmittedDate for a missing/invalid IncidentDate.
- Preserves the structured SubmittedDate timestamp and adds SubmittedDateOnly
  without timezone conversion. Confirm timestamp semantics before day-level use.
- Extracts semicolon/newline-separated USB, Access keys / badges and
  Documents / drawings quantities from LostOther. Other labels are flagged.
- Allows only nonnegative integer quantities; preserves nulls and explicit zeros.
- For USB: uses structured quantity if present and valid; otherwise recognized
  LostOther quantity. If both agree, counts once. If they disagree, leaves the
  resolved USB quantity null and flags the conflict. Repeated USB labels are flagged.
- KnownLostAssetCount sums available valid PC, phone, USB, badge and document
  quantities. It is a partial recorded quantity, not a certified total. Unknown
  types, missing quantities, and conflicts can make it incomplete.
- HasRecordedAssetLoss indicates recognized LostItems selections or a positive
  parsed quantity; it does not classify every security-incident type.

IMPORTANT LIMITS BEFORE EXECUTIVE REPORTING
- The observed snapshot example had Incident Date: 20206-02-09. Determine whether
  this was a transcription typo or exists in the source. Do not automatically
  replace it with 2026. Incident Time also looked like a date; it is not used.
- The snapshot is upstream context and may be less curated than the reporting
  columns. Parsed dates/locations still require validation by the data owners.
- Null asset quantities are not converted to zero. Confirm whether nonselected
  assets imply zero and whether unknown selected-asset quantities can be filled.
- Never add parsed LostOther USB quantities to the structured USB column blindly.
- Documents, badges, phones and PCs can be shown separately. Agree on what belongs
  in a combined lost-asset metric before making it a headline KPI.
- All reporting-ready incidents remain available, including foreign locations
  involving US subsidiaries. Geographic country and organizational scope differ.
- April fiscal start, FY naming convention, and period completeness cutoff are
  pending. This package intentionally contains no FY labels or YoY formulas yet.
- GCE SPS is not assumed to be an authoritative department name.
- ReporterEmail is a reporter identifier, not a verified identifier for User at
  Fault. Do not use it to attach the reporter's department to the at-fault person.
- This package does not implement historical snapshots, refresh scheduling,
  permissions, final dimension tables, visuals, or source replacement.
